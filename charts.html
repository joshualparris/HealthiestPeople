Health Connect + scale

What this means

The useful conclusion is not “keep losing weight”. Your current plan is deliberately about maintaining around the high-70-kg range while building strength and function.

Interpretation

The recorded ferritin value rose from 35 to 41 µg/L between January 2025 and February 2026. That is a small trend, not a diagnosis or proof that iron stores are “optimal”; future interpretation belongs with the broader iron studies and clinical context.

Sleep trend

Weekly restful sleep

Fitbit / Google Health

The early-2026 record contains several short-sleep weeks, followed by a much stronger run of roughly 7½–9 hours in the later Google Health weeks. The data source changes, so the chart marks that transition rather than pretending every point is perfectly equivalent.

What changed

Recent sleep duration is no longer the obvious weak point it was earlier in the year. The plan now says protect this improvement rather than piling on more sleep optimisation.

Decision aids

Diagrams that explain what to do with health data

The useful-data loop

Measure→Notice a trend→Ask what decision changes→Act or ignore→Re-check later

If a measurement cannot change a sensible decision, it may not deserve ongoing attention.

The evidence ladder

AIndependent peer-reviewed / institutional
BIndependently reported clinical / lab result
CFirst-party published measurement
DSelf-report / interview

Lower letters do not make information useless; they change how confidently it should be interpreted.

The ordinary-family filter

1Does it have plausible benefit?
2Can it fit work and family?
3Is the cost reasonable?
4Does it need medical supervision?
5Is the likely benefit big enough to matter?

That filter is why sleep, movement, strength, food and basic preventive care stay ahead of expensive longevity extras.

What survives across the case studies

The ordinary-person stack

1

Move most days

Walking counts. So do family outings, cycling, running, chores and active transport.

2

Strength twice a week

Squat, hinge, push, pull and carry. Preserve useful capacity, not gym status.

3

Build aerobic fitness

Easy volume plus some harder work. Improve your own baseline rather than chasing elite numbers.

4

Protect sleep

Adequate sleep opportunity and repeatable timing are foundational.

5

Eat mostly ordinary food

Plants, fibre and practical protein beat branded longevity food.

6

Measure with a question

Track things that can change a decision. More data is not automatically better.

7

Follow persistent signals

Repeated abnormalities deserve sensible clinical follow-up.

8

Keep relationships inside the plan

A health protocol that regularly costs family connection, rest or peace is badly designed.
